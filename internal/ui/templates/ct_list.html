The state is that of the newest certificate of each set of names. Renewal is expected at two thirds of the lifetime; overdue means less than seven days (or the last tenth of the lifetime) are left and no newer certificate exists; replaced means every name is in a newer certificate of another set. Unexpected CA means the zone's CAA records, as they are now, do not allow the issuing CA. "Via the broker" is matched by serial number; Certificate Transparency does not show which ACME account was used.
